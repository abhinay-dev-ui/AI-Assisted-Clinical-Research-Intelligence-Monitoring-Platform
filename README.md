# AI-Assisted-Clinical-Research-Intelligence-Monitoring-Platform

Clinical research organizations accumulate study information across protocols, study records, progress updates, incidents, deviations, reports, and historical research. Researchers need to find relevant information quickly, while senior stakeholders need visibility into study progress and significant deviations.
Traditional search and dashboards have two limitations:
- information is distributed and difficult to explore conversationally;
- monitoring systems can identify numbers or rule violations but often do not explain the surrounding evidence clearly.
Our platform will combine deterministic enterprise systems with GenAI so that:
AI retrieves, summarizes, compares and explains.
Application code controls authorization, calculations and rules.
Humans make consequential decisions.
